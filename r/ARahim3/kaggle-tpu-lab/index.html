kaggle-tpu-lab

by ARahim3

Frontier-class open models on a free Kaggle TPU v5e-8: GLM-5.3-Flash 320B MoE (~165 tok/s, our own JAX engine) and Qwen3.8-27B bf16 (~130 tok/s), 262k context, prefix caching. Works with Claude Code, Codex, opencode and pi.
