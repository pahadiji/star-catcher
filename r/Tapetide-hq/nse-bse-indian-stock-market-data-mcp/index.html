nse-bse-indian-stock-market-data-mcp

by Tapetide-hq

Indian stock market MCP server for NSE & BSE — 48 tools (55 with aliases) for about 8,000 stocks: live quotes, financials, technicals, 326-ratio screener, FII/DII flows, option chains & IV, filings, IPOs & portfolio. Remote (OAuth) or npx. Works with Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, Codex & Gemini
